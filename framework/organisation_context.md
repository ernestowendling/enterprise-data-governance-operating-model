# Organisation Context

## HelvetiaCare Insurance Group

HelvetiaCare Insurance Group is a fictional Swiss health and insurance services organisation created exclusively for this portfolio project.

The organisation provides insurance products and healthcare-related services to individual and corporate customers across Switzerland.

All organisations, roles, systems, processes and data used in this repository are fictional or synthetic.

## Business Context

HelvetiaCare uses data to support:

- Customer onboarding and servicing
- Policy administration
- Claims processing
- Healthcare-provider management
- Financial reporting
- Regulatory reporting
- Business analytics
- Artificial intelligence use cases

The organisation is expanding its use of data analytics, automation and artificial intelligence. It therefore requires a consistent enterprise Data Governance operating model.

## Governance Challenge

Data responsibilities have historically been distributed across business units and technology teams.

This has resulted in:

- Inconsistent data definitions
- Unclear ownership and accountability
- Different data-quality practices across departments
- Limited visibility over critical data elements
- Fragmented issue-management processes
- Inconsistent metadata
- Limited evidence that governance requirements are operationalised

The organisation has therefore established an enterprise Data Governance programme sponsored by the Chief Data Officer.

## Programme Objectives

The Data Governance programme aims to:

1. Establish clear ownership and stewardship responsibilities.
2. Create common governance principles and standards.
3. Identify and govern critical data elements.
4. Improve data quality and metadata completeness.
5. Define transparent decision and escalation processes.
6. Integrate governance requirements into operational processes and tools.
7. Support responsible analytics, automation and artificial intelligence.
8. Measure governance effectiveness through defined indicators and maturity assessments.

## Regulatory Environment

HelvetiaCare operates in a regulated environment involving sensitive customer, financial and health-related information.

The governance model must therefore support:

- Data protection
- Information security
- Access management
- Data retention
- Regulatory compliance
- Auditability
- Risk management
- Responsible data and AI usage

This repository does not provide legal advice. Its controls and standards are illustrative examples of an enterprise governance framework.

## Governance Scope

The initial governance scope covers five enterprise data domains:

1. Customer
2. Policy
3. Claims
4. Healthcare Provider
5. Finance

Each domain has an accountable Data Owner, an operational Data Steward and supporting Data Custodians.
